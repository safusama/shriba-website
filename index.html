The place

A small studio for sound and craft.

In the heart of Thamel, Shriba is a place to meet Himalayan singing bowls up close.

Explore hand-beaten brass bowls, learn how to hold and play one, or settle in for a one-to-one sound session. We keep the experience personal, unhurried, and grounded in the craft.

A little time to learn, practice, and listen.

A lifelong practice

A tradition learned at home, shared around the world.

For 30 years, Saroj Shakya has practiced and taught Himalayan singing bowl work, learning through family tradition.

At Shriba in Thamel, he welcomes visitors to learn through hands-on workshops and one-to-one sound sessions. His approach is practical and personal: discover how to hold, play, and listen to a bowl at your own pace.

Rooted in Nepal. Shared through teaching, one bowl at a time.

The craft

Made by hand. Shared with care.

The bowls we share are hand-beaten by Kathmandu artisans, with craft knowledge carried across generations. At Shriba, that living tradition continues through the simple acts of making, teaching, and listening.

Come learn the practice at your own pace. We share the sound and the craft without promising cures.
