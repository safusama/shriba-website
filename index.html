About Shriba

Warm, handmade, heritage.

Shriba Singing Bowl Centre is a small workshop and practice space in Thamel, Kathmandu. Every bowl we sell is hand-beaten from brass by artisans we know — the same families who have made them for generations.

We offer workshops for visitors who want to learn the practice, one-to-one therapy sessions, and a quiet space to sit with the sound. Our approach is plain-spoken and unhurried.

  • Hand-beaten Himalayan bowls
  • Workshops & therapy sessions
  • Rooted in Kathmandu heritage

Brass warmth, hand-held, close crops.

What we do

◎

Workshops

Learn to hold, strike and circle a Himalayan singing bowl. Small groups, patient pace, no prior experience needed.

Therapy Sessions

One-to-one sessions on the floor, using bowls placed on and around the body. Soft light, textiles, unhurried.

Bowls & Craft

Hand-beaten bowls for practice, export and retail. Come see how they are made and pick the one that sounds right.
